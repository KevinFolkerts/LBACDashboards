    Net billed vs. collected, by year

    Net billed = facility fees at rate card − discounts/concessions + program add-ons + cancellation fees + damages. Collected = payments actually received (credit card, check, cash, EFT, program-account transfers), net of refunds. 2026 includes stays booked through December.

    Facility utilization

    Weekend utilization = Friday + Saturday nights used on bookable weekends ÷ bookable Friday + Saturday nights (82 per year = 52 weekends less the 11 blackout weekends listed in 205), counted only from the date each facility became reservable — so the campsites added in Jul–Aug 2026 (#04–#15) and the Program Cabin (Aug 2025) are measured against their short windows, not against years they didn't exist ("since" dates shown by the name). Nights used on blackout weekends (council programs such as Webelos Weekend, Memorial Day Family Camp, the Tahquitz Community Labor Day weekend) are shown separately and excluded from the ratio. 2026 counts a full year of availability, so its share will rise as fall bookings land. Cabins sleep 9; the Lodge seats 106.

    Blackout weekends — not available for year-round rental

    Per council direction: eight consecutive weekends of summer-camp exclusive use — the staff-training weekend, the six camp weekends (from the weekend before Session 1 through the weekend after the last session) and Webelos Weekend — plus Memorial Day, Labor Day and Thanksgiving weekends. Webelos Weekend dates come from the Webelos Weekend exports (2024–26) and the 2023 reservation; the camp weekends are anchored to them (2025–26 session dates confirmed from the summer camp exports). Only Friday and Saturday nights are counted, so 52 weekends − 11 = 41 bookable weekends = 82 Fri/Sat nights per facility per year.

    Cabin capacity rules — audit

    Per council rules: a cabin sleeps 9, there are only 8 cabins per night, and when a registration lists more people than fit in the cabins it booked, cabins are added for those nights — but only cabins that were actually free (never exceeding 8, never double-booking). Added nights carry $0 since billing was correct as charged; they count in usage and utilization. Applies to stays whose only sleeping facilities are cabins (campsite stays excluded — those people have tent capacity). This audit is not affected by the filters.

    Data sources, definitions and caveats

    Sources

    • Reservation Data.csv (as of —) — every reservation on the account: customer, dates, estimated attendance, reserved facility-dates at rate-card price, and Black Pug's money columns.
    • Reservation_Financial_Detail_Report_<date>.csv — the transaction ledger for account 1-6701-760-21 (latest export wins). All billed/collected figures come from it.
    • Reservations_by_Booking_Date_<date>.csv — booking date per reservation (reservations it does not cover — mostly cancelled or system-era stays — fall back to their first ledger transaction). Lead times use this.
    • Facility usage by Day_<year>.csv — current-year daily grid; it matches the reserved-facility dates parsed from the reservation export exactly (facility by facility), which is the cross-check that the multi-year facility-night counts are right.
    • FacilityPricing.csv, Facility_Information_Pricing.csv, Facility Amenities.csv — rate card, capacities and amenities.

    Definitions

    • Facility-night — one facility reserved for one calendar date (Black Pug counts each reserved date as a night; a Friday–Sunday stay is 2). Range, amphitheater and day-use bookings count each reserved date/block the same way.
    • Gross fees — facility fees at the rate card, net of reversals for cancelled/changed dates. Discounts & concessions — "Discount Reservation Cost" plus "Miscellaneous Credit" adjustments (price matches, service-work credits, weather/septic credits). Add-ons — "Miscellaneous Price Change" and per-person lines (archery, BB, rifle/shotgun, climbing, lake, commissary, propane). Net billed = gross + discounts + add-ons + cancellation/forfeit fees + damages.
    • Collected — cash received (merchant credit card, credit card at office, check, cash, EFT, program-account transfers, unit account) plus customer credits applied from other reservations, minus refunds and credits moved out. Balance = net billed − collected (positive = still owed).
    • Segments — LBAC units = Long Beach Area Council units (Iron Star district) and LBAC groups; Out-of-council BSA = units from other councils; Non-BSA groups = churches, civic groups, schools, clubs; Council programs & internal = council-run events (project-coded reservations) and staff/volunteer holds with no unit or group attached.

    Caveats

    • The current year is partial: stays through — have happened; later stays and next-year bookings are included as "upcoming". Use the pacing tables (106/107) for year-over-year comparisons. Next year's blackout dates are estimated until camp dates are published.
    • Attendance is the estimated headcount entered on the reservation; actual check-in counts exist for only 14 reservations. Guest-nights = estimated headcount × nights.
    • Council-event bookings are priced at rate card and then discounted (e.g. Memorial Day Family Camp 2024: $51,600 list → $500 net), which inflates gross/discount totals when the council segment is included.
    • Outstanding balances include a $10,220 forfeit fee on a 2023 cancelled Crew 228 reservation that was never collected, and council-internal holds that are typically zeroed out or paid by program accounts.
    • Cabin capacity rules (see the audit in 610): cabin bookings are capped at 9 people per cabin and 8 cabins per night. One export artifact was corrected — 14LP7 (Troop 241, Jan 2025) listed each of its 4 cabins twice; the 8 phantom cabin-nights were removed (the ledger billed 8, $720). 39 cabin-nights across 11 stays were added where the estimated headcount exceeded 9 × cabins booked and cabins were free those nights; 9 stays still exceed capacity even with every available cabin (their estimates likely include day visitors or tenting outside the system).
    • Reservation 15FDS — Pack 78's Dec 5–6, 2025 cabin weekend — was booked as 8 separate registrations (one per cabin, incl. 15DG5 entered under "Member of LBAC"). Costs and facilities were correct, just spread across registrations, so they are merged into one stay here (Cabins 1–8, 125 est. people, $1,440). Reservation counts are therefore 7 lower than raw Black Pug.
    • Reservation 15RI0 — Monster Mountain, Oct 2026 — is a council-event facility hold; since the Aug 20, 2026 ledger Black Pug itself carries it at $0 (a −$7,050 price change), matching the earlier manual adjustment. Its facility-nights still count as upcoming council usage.
    • Newer inventory has little history: Program Cabin, the ranges and the amphitheater first appear in 2025–26; Tent Campsites #04, #06, #08, #09, #10, #13 and #15 were added Jul–Aug 2026 (#15's first booking is Oct 2026; #08, #09, #10 and #13 are not yet booked); the Kiwanis Apartment appears only in the amenities export (not reservable, not on the rate card).
